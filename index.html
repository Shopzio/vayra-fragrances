Need to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add your chosen products to the bag and continue to checkout. In this demo, checkout creates an order enquiry that you can copy or send to WhatsApp after adding your own business number.

When will my order arrive?

Delivery timelines depend on the supplier and your PIN code. Add the verified delivery estimate from your supplier to each listing before launching.

Are all perfumes authentic?

Only list products whose authenticity and source you can verify. Do not describe a product as branded, original or authentic unless you can substantiate that claim.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Your final return and exchange policy must match the supplier's actual terms and applicable consumer rules. Publish your exact policy before taking orders.

How can I pay?

UPI/payment gateway is not connected in this website demo. Before going live, configure a suitable payment method and display clear payment and refund instructions.
